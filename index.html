Aton Kamanda

I work on AI safety at LawZero, the nonprofit founded by Yoshua Bengio, where we are building Scientist AI: a non-agentic system that aims to understand and explain the world rather than act in it, so that it can serve as a trustworthy guardrail for more autonomous AI.

Before that, I spent my time turning research into working systems: LLM agents for legal translation at Alexa Translations, and real-time video understanding at AwakeAI, a Mila-incubated startup. I studied artificial intelligence at Mila and the Université de Montréal, and computer science at the University of Namur.
